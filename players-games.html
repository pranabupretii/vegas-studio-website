    Reading the numbers

    How to read RTP and volatility

    Two figures appear on every card. Here is what they do and do not tell you.

    95%

    RTP: a long-run average

    Return to player is the share of all money staked that a game is designed to pay back across a very large number of rounds. An RTP of 95% does not mean you get 95 back from 100 in a session. Short-term results can sit far above or below it, and RTP never predicts an individual result.

    Some games list more than one RTP. The operator chooses which setting to offer, so check the game's information screen where you play.

    RTP in the glossary
    LowMediumHighVery high

    Volatility: how wins are spread

    Lower volatility usually means smaller wins more often. Higher volatility usually means longer gaps between wins, with the chance of larger ones. It describes a pattern over many rounds, not what happens next, and it says nothing about whether a game is "due".

    Where a game page does not publish volatility, we leave it off the card rather than guess.
