iGaming glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms you will meet across games, platforms and promotions.

ABFHJKLMOPRSVW

A

Aggregator
A company that bundles games from many providers and delivers them to operators through one integration.
API
Application programming interface: the technical connection that lets an operator’s platform launch games, manage sessions and handle wallet transactions.

B

B2B
Business to business. Studios like Vegas Studio supply games and tools to operators rather than to players directly.
Bonus round
A special feature inside a game, triggered by certain symbols or events, that plays differently from the base game.
Buy feature
An option in some games that lets a player pay to enter the bonus round directly. Availability depends on market rules.

F

Free spins
Spins that do not cost the player a stake. They can be a feature inside a game or a reward given through a promotion.

H

Hit rate
How often, on average, a spin produces any win. A hit rate of 25% means roughly one winning spin in four over the long run.
House edge
The theoretical share of stakes a game keeps over time. It is 100% minus the RTP.

J

Jackpot
A large prize, either fixed or progressive. A progressive jackpot grows as players contribute to it until it is won.

K

Keno
A number game where players pick numbers and win according to how many match the numbers drawn.

L

Lobby
The part of a casino site or app where players browse and choose games.

M

Max win
The largest possible win in a single game round, usually shown as a multiple of the stake.
Multiplier
A factor that increases a win, such as 2x or 5x.

O

Operator
A licensed company that runs a casino site or app and offers games to players.

P

Paylines
Fixed patterns across the reels on which matching symbols create a win.
Promo tools
Features such as tournaments, challenges, free spins and jackpots that operators use to run campaigns.

R

Reels
The vertical columns of symbols in a slot game.
Responsible gaming
Policies and tools that help players stay in control, such as limits, time-outs and self-exclusion.
RNG
Random number generator: the software that makes every game outcome independent and unpredictable.
RTP
Return to player: the theoretical share of all stakes a game pays back over a very large number of rounds. Short-term results can differ widely.

S

Scatter
A symbol that pays or triggers a feature wherever it lands, not only on a payline.

V

Volatility
How a game’s wins are spread. Low volatility means smaller, more frequent wins; high volatility means larger, less frequent wins.

W

Ways to win
A system where matching symbols on adjacent reels win in any position, instead of along fixed paylines.
Wild
A symbol that substitutes for others to help complete a win.
