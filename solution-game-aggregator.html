Running the lobby

One back office for every studio's games

Content from different providers behaves the same once it is in. Your content team works in one place instead of logging in to a dozen provider portals.

  • Sort and feature

    Pin titles to the top of a category, build themed shelves such as "New this week" and reorder by drag and drop.

  • Market rules

    Set which games appear in which market. A title that is not approved somewhere is hidden there automatically.

  • Scheduled releases

    Queue a new game to appear at a set time, so launches line up with your newsletter or push campaign.

  • Free rounds across studios

    Issue free rounds on any supported game from one screen, with the same rules whichever provider made it.

  • Tags and filters

    Tag games by theme, feature or volatility so players can filter the lobby the way they think about games.

  • Performance view

    See bets, wins and active players per game and per provider, side by side, in one report.

Questions

Game aggregation, answered

Something we have not covered? Ask the team and an integration engineer will reply.

How many providers can I access?

The connection carries a growing list of studios across slots, table games, live casino and instant games, plus the full Vegas Studio catalogue. The exact list depends on your markets, so we share it after a first call.

Do I have to take every provider?

No. You choose providers, categories and individual games. Many operators start with a focused lobby and add studios as they learn what their players like.

How long does integration take?

A direct API build typically takes four to eight weeks, depending on your team and platform. If your platform already carries our aggregation, it can be a matter of days.

Which wallet models do you support?

We support a single wallet, where balances stay on your platform, and a transfer wallet for setups that need it. Our engineers will recommend one after looking at your platform.

How do you handle different markets?

Each game carries the markets it is approved for. The back office hides anything that is not available where a player is, and you can add your own rules on top.

Can I run free rounds and tournaments across providers?

Free rounds work across every studio that supports them, from one screen. Tournaments and other promotions can be added through our marketing tools.

What does it cost?

Pricing is a revenue share on the content you use, with no separate integration fee for the standard API. We will give you a clear proposal once we know your markets and volumes.
