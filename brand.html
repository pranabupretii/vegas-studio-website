White VEGAS. Hot pink STUDIO. Keep the supplied transparent artwork intact and proportional. The logo is designed for dark grounds, and sits directly on an approved plum field.

04

Logo do and don't

The examples marked "Don't" are deliberately wrong, made here with CSS so you can recognise the mistakes. No black backgrounds behind the company name. No black lettering in the company name. No green boxes behind the logo.

  • Do

    Supplied artwork, unchanged, on plum with clear space.

  • Do

    On light pages, inside a plum field.

  • Don't stretch

    Never squash or stretch. Scale both directions together.

  • Don't recolour

    White VEGAS and pink STUDIO only. No new colour schemes.

  • Don't use busy backgrounds

    Game art behind the logo makes it unreadable. Use a plum field.

  • Don't use black

    No black backgrounds behind the company name.

  • Don't box it in green

    No green boxes behind the logo, ever.

  • Don't rotate or add effects

    No tilts, glows, outlines or drop shadows.

05

Imagery

Our best pictures are our games. Use official key art from the catalogue, let the characters lead, and keep the frame clean.

Keep key art clean

Use the cover as supplied. No filters, no shouty text over the characters, no promises of winning.

Crop to the character

Keep faces and the game title in frame. If a crop cuts through the hero, choose the wide banner instead.

  • Use art from the official game pages; ask us for higher-resolution files.
  • Show the game name next to its art, spelled as in the catalogue.
  • Player-facing material always carries 18+ and safer-play messaging. See responsible gaming.
  • No stock photos of cash, cards or people celebrating wins.
06

Voice and tone

We sound like a studio that knows its craft: plain, specific and warm. Louder for a launch, calmer for a player, but always the same people talking. UK spelling, sentence-case headings.

  • PlainShort words, short sentences. If a ten-year-old would stumble, rewrite it.
  • SpecificName the feature, the number, the date. Facts beat adjectives.
  • Warm, not loudEnthusiastic about games, never pushy about play.
  • ResponsibleNo promises of winning, no pressure, no urgency tricks.

Game launch

Write like this

Buffalo Quest is coming soon: sticky wilds on 20 paylines, with RTP configurations of 96.5%, 97% and 98%.

Not like this

Get ready for the most epic, game-changing slot experience of the year!!!

Operator email

Write like this

The new keno build is on staging. Two things changed: draw speed and the results screen. Notes attached.

Not like this

We're thrilled to share some exciting updates that will take your keno offering to the next level.

Player-facing

Write like this

Fairy's Wish is now in the Players Hub. 18+. Set a limit before you play.

Not like this

Your lucky day is here. Spin now before the magic runs out!

Social post

Write like this

Steam Strikes: gears, steam and a lot of brass. See the game page for specs.

Not like this

Unleash the power of steam and supercharge your wins today.

Questions and approvals

Not sure? Ask before you publish. It is quicker than a reprint.

Can partners use the Vegas Studio logo?

Operators, affiliates and media partners can use the supplied logo to show they carry or cover our games, following this guide. Do not use it in a way that suggests we endorse your brand or run your casino.

Can I use game art in my lobby or article?

Yes, if you carry the game or are writing about it. Use official art, name the game correctly and keep player-facing material 18+ with safer-play messaging. Media partners can find more on the media partners page.

Is there a version of the logo for white backgrounds?

The approved artwork has white VEGAS lettering, so on white or paper place it inside a plum field. Please do not recolour the lettering to make a "dark" version.

Who approves brand use?

Send a draft through the press route on our contact page. Tell us where it will appear and when.
