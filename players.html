Responsible play

Play for fun, on your terms

Players Hub and our games are for adults aged 18 and over, or older where local law says so. Every game result is random. Quests, XP and leaderboards are there to make the hub more fun to explore, never a reason to play longer than you planned.

  • Decide how long you will play before you start
  • Set a budget you are comfortable losing
  • Take regular breaks away from the screen
  • Never chase losses

What is Players Hub?

A place to explore games, follow community activity and track your progress. Sign in on the official hub for quests, rewards and available early-access demos.

What is RTP?

Theoretical return to player describes a game’s expected return across a very large number of plays. Short-term observed RTP can differ significantly. Neither predicts an individual result. See how it works on the Live RTP page.

Can I preview upcoming games?

The official Early Access section lists available previews and demos. Eligibility, access and any rewards depend on the activity’s terms.

What is the Daily Reward?

The official hub offers eligible account holders a daily reward feature. Sign in to check the current requirements and available rewards.

How do I earn XP?

Eligible activities on the official hub can award experience points, including quests and events. Your account shows the current tasks and progress.
