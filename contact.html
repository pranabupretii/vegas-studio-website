Good questions

Contact, answered

Something else? Talk to the studio.

How quickly do you reply?

We aim to reply to business enquiries within two working days. Typical times for each desk are in the table above.

Can we arrange a call?

Yes. Mention it in your enquiry and we will suggest times.

Why is there no form on this page?

All enquiries go through the official Vegas Studio contact page so they reach one inbox and nothing gets lost. This page helps you choose the route and prepare what to say.

I have a problem with my casino account. Can you help?

Your account, balance and payments are held by the casino you play at, so their support team is the right place. If the issue is with one of our games, tell us the game name and the casino.

Can I request a demo of a game?

Operators can ask for demo builds in their enquiry. Players can find game information and demos through the Players Hub and each game page.

Do you attend events?

Yes. See the events page, and mention the event in your enquiry if you would like to meet there.
