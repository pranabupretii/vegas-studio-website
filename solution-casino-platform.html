Stop 1 of 6 · Dashboard

  1. 01/ 06 · Dashboard

    Start the day with the numbers that matter

    The dashboard opens on today's trading: revenue, deposits, active players and sign-ups, each compared with the same hour last week. A live feed underneath shows big wins, large deposits and anything flagged for review.

    • Hourly comparisons against last week and last month
    • Alerts for unusual wins, deposits or bonus use
    • One view per brand, or every brand together

  2. 02/ 06 · Games

    Shape the lobby without a developer

    Every game from Vegas Studio and the aggregation sits in one library. Filter by provider, category or market, then switch titles on, hide them or schedule them for later. Changes reach the live lobby in under a minute.

    • Drag-and-drop shelves such as "New" and "Jackpots"
    • Market rules hide games that are not approved locally
    • Per-game RTP configuration where the game supports it

  3. 03/ 06 · Players

    Know each player, and look after them

    Search any account to see balance, transactions, bonuses, verification status and safer gambling limits on one screen. Support agents can answer most questions without switching tools, and every action they take is logged.

    • Identity checks and document review in the account view
    • Deposit, loss and session limits set by the player or by you
    • Segments built from behaviour, value and lifecycle stage

  4. 04/ 06 · Promotions

    Build a campaign in the time it takes to write the email

    Set up free rounds, deposit matches, cashback, prize wheels and tournaments from templates. Choose who qualifies, cap the cost and set wagering rules, then watch claims and spend as the campaign runs.

    • Free rounds on any supported game, from any provider
    • Budget caps that pause a campaign automatically
    • Prize wheels and gift drops from our promo games

  5. 05/ 06 · Reports

    Reports your finance team will trust

    Revenue, bonus cost, payments and game performance, all built from the same transaction ledger, so the numbers in a marketing report match the numbers in a finance one. Schedule exports or connect your own BI tool.

    • Revenue by brand, market, game, provider and segment
    • Scheduled CSV exports and a read-only reporting API
    • Figures in your currency and time zone

  6. 06/ 06 · Settings

    Give every team the access they need, and no more

    Create roles for support, CRM, finance and compliance, each with its own permissions. Two-factor sign-in can be made compulsory, and an audit trail records who changed what, and when.

    • Role-based permissions down to single actions
    • Compulsory two-factor sign-in for staff accounts
    • Full audit trail, searchable and exportable

Module deep-dives

What sits behind the screens

Seven modules make up the platform. They share one player record and one ledger, which is why a bonus, a deposit and a limit all show up in the same place.

Module 01

Cashier and payments

Deposits and withdrawals through the payment providers you choose, with routing rules that send each transaction to the method most likely to succeed.

  • Cards, bank transfers, e-wallets and local methods
  • Routing and fallback rules per market and currency
  • Withdrawal approval queues with risk scoring
  • Automatic reconciliation against provider reports

Works withPlayersReportsRisk

Module 02

Player accounts and verification

Registration, sign-in and identity checks built around the rules of each market you operate in.

  • Configurable sign-up forms per market
  • Document upload and manual review queue
  • Age and identity checks through your chosen provider
  • Self-service account area for players

Works withCashierSafer gambling

Module 03

Safer gambling

Tools that help players stay in control and help your team spot when someone might need support.

  • Deposit, loss, wager and session limits
  • Time-outs and self-exclusion with cooling-off periods
  • Reality checks during play
  • Behaviour markers that raise a case for review

Works withPlayersCRM

Module 04

Bonus engine

One engine for every bonus type, so wagering, expiry and game weighting work the same way everywhere.

  • Free rounds, deposit matches, cashback and free bets
  • Game weighting and maximum bet rules
  • Bonus wallets kept separate from cash
  • Cost tracking per campaign and per player

Works withPromotionsReports

Module 05

CRM and messaging

Segment players and reach them by email, SMS, push or on-site message, triggered by what they do.

  • Segments from behaviour, value and lifecycle
  • Triggered journeys such as welcome and win-back
  • On-site inbox and pop-ups
  • Suppression rules for excluded players

Works withPlayersBonus engine

Module 06

Risk and fraud

Rules and scoring that catch bonus abuse, multi-accounting and payment fraud before they cost you money.

  • Device and payment fingerprinting
  • Linked-account detection
  • Configurable rules with case management
  • Velocity checks on deposits and withdrawals

Works withCashierPlayers

Module 07

Content and lobby

The full game library, lobby layout and on-site content managed from one place, per brand and per market.

  • Vegas Studio games plus the aggregation
  • Lobby shelves, banners and landing pages
  • Market rules for game availability
  • Multi-language content and translations

Questions

Casino platform, answered

01Can I use my own brand and design?

Yes. The player-facing lobby is themed to your brand, and you can run several brands with different looks from one back office. If you have your own front end, it can talk to the platform through our API.

02Which games are included?

The full Vegas Studio catalogue, plus content from the providers on our game aggregation. You choose what goes live in each market.

03Can I bring my own payment providers?

Yes. We connect the providers you already have contracts with, and can recommend others for new markets.

04How long does a launch take?

A first brand typically takes eight to twelve weeks, depending on markets, payment providers and how quickly licences and contracts come through.

05Where is the platform hosted?

In cloud regions chosen to meet the data rules of your markets. We confirm hosting during scoping.

06Do you help after launch?

Yes. You get an account manager, round-the-clock technical monitoring and regular reviews of performance and new features.

07How is it priced?

Usually a set-up fee and a revenue share, with the details depending on markets and volumes. We will put a clear proposal together after a first call.
