Custom games

Bring us a brand, a character or a half-formed idea. Our artists, mathematicians and developers turn it into a finished slot or keno game that feels like it could only belong to you.

Two ways in

Re-skin a proven game, or start from a blank page

Most projects take one of two routes. Both end with a game that carries your name; the difference is how much of the engine underneath is new.

The storyboard

Seven frames from brief to launch

Scroll along the board to follow a new build. You sign off each frame before the next one starts, so there are no surprises at the end.

  1. Frame 01 Week 1

    Brief

    We talk through your brand, players and markets, and agree what success looks like.

    • Audience and market notes
    • Reference games you like
    • Scope and budget

  2. Frame 02 Weeks 1 to 2

    Moodboard

    Colour, texture and character references pinned to one board, so everyone agrees on the feel.

    • Two or three visual directions
    • Palette and type ideas
    • Sound references

  3. Frame 03 Weeks 2 to 4

    Sketches

    Pencil roughs of the logo, main symbols and screen layout. Cheap to change, so this is the time to be picky.

    • Logo and symbol roughs
    • Screen layout for mobile and desktop
    • Feature flow on paper

  4. Frame 04 Weeks 2 to 6

    Maths model

    Our mathematicians shape how the game pays: hit rate, volatility, feature frequency and RTP options.

    • Paytable and reel strips
    • Simulation results
    • RTP variants for each market

  5. Frame 05 Weeks 4 to 12

    Art and animation

    Final artwork, symbol animations, big-win sequences and the sound to go with them.

    • Every symbol in final colour
    • Win and feature animations
    • Music and sound effects

  6. Frame 06 Weeks 8 to 16

    Build and test

    Developers put it all together. Testers play it on phones, tablets and desktops until nothing surprises them.

    • Playable builds every sprint
    • Device and browser testing
    • Documentation for your compliance team

  7. Frame 07 Week 16 onwards

    Launch

    The game goes live in your lobby with launch artwork, and we watch the first weeks with you.

    • Lobby tiles and banners
    • Launch campaign support
    • Performance review after 30 days

The crew

Who works on your game

Like the credits at the end of a film, except you meet everyone at the start. One producer is your contact from the brief to launch day.

Producer
Runs the schedule, chairs every review and is your single point of contact.
Game designer
Shapes the features, the bonus rounds and how the game feels in the hand.
Mathematician
Builds the model, runs millions of simulated spins and prepares RTP variants.
Concept artist
Draws the sketches and moodboards that set the look.
2D artist and animator
Paints final symbols and backgrounds, then brings them to life.
Sound designer
Writes the music and the small sounds that make a win feel like one.
Developer
Builds the game client and connects it to your platform.
QA tester
Plays every build on real devices and checks the maths matches the screen.

Questions

Custom games, answered

Something else? Talk to the studio.

Can you adapt an existing game?

Yes. Re-skinning a proven game is often the fastest route, because the maths and the core features are already tested.

Can the game be exclusive to us?

Exclusive arrangements can be discussed with the studio, for a period of time or for good, and for some or all of your markets.

How long does a custom game take?

It depends on the scope. As a guide, a re-skin takes a couple of months and a new build takes longer. We share a firm timeline after the sketch stage.

Who owns the artwork?

Your brand assets stay yours. Ownership of the new game artwork is agreed in the contract before production starts.

How many rounds of changes do we get?

Each frame of the storyboard has a review and a round of changes built in. Changes are cheapest at the sketch stage, so we encourage big feedback early.

Can you build keno as well as slots?

Yes. Our keno titles, such as Super Hexa Keno, are built by the same team, and the same process applies.

Got a sketch on a napkin?

That is enough to start. Send us what you have and we will reply with questions, a rough scope and the next step.
