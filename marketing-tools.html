    03 · Campaign recipes

    Four campaigns we set up again and again

    Tools work best in combination. These are the pairings operators ask for most, with the settings we usually start from.

    Fri 18:00 to Sun 23:59

    Weekend warm-up

    • Tournament
    • Free spins
    1. Open a points tournament on three slots on Friday evening.
    2. Send 10 free spins on Saturday morning to anyone outside the top 50, so they can climb back in.
    3. Pay out the top 20 on Monday and show everyone their final rank.

    Best for lifting weekend play without a big prize budget.

    7 days

    Welcome back

    • Free spins
    • Challenge
    1. Target players who have not visited for 30 to 90 days.
    2. Greet them with free spins on a game they played before.
    3. Follow with a three-step challenge that rewards a return on day 3 and day 7.

    Best for turning a one-off return into a habit again.

    14 days around release

    New game launch

    • Challenge
    • Free spins
    • Tournament
    1. Before launch, show a teaser tile with the release date.
    2. Launch day: free spins on the new title for active players.
    3. Week two: a challenge to trigger the bonus feature, then a short tournament to close.

    Best for getting players past the first five spins of a new game.

    One evening, monthly

    Jackpot night

    • Jackpot
    • Challenge

    06 · Ground rules

    Campaigns players can trust

    A promotion only helps if players understand it. These rules are switched on for every campaign we run with you.

    1. Terms on screen. Eligibility, end date and any wagering conditions sit one tap away inside the game.
    2. Excluded players stay excluded. Self-excluded players and anyone on a cool-off period are filtered out before a campaign starts.
    3. Caps you control. Set a budget ceiling and a per-player limit. The campaign stops itself when either is reached.
    4. No pressure timers. Countdowns show when something ends, never a fake “last chance” that resets.
    5. Market by market. Settings follow the rules of each market you run in, and tools that are not allowed there stay off.

    07 · Questions

    Marketing tools, answered

    Something else? Talk to the studio.

    Can I target specific players?

    Yes. Campaigns can be aimed at segments such as new or returning players, a market, a VIP level or players who like a certain game type. Excluded players are always left out.

    Do the tools work with all games?

    They work with Vegas Studio slots and keno games. If you want a campaign that also covers other content in your lobby, talk to us about what is possible.

    How far ahead can I schedule?

    As far as you like. Campaigns can be set up with fixed start and end times, so a December plan can be ready in October.

    Can several campaigns run at once?

    Yes. We suggest no more than one tool per game at a time, so players are never unsure which offer they are playing for.

    How do I see results?

    You can watch participation while a campaign is live, and you get a summary report when it ends, including a comparison group where the numbers allow it.

    Can campaigns carry our branding?

    Yes. Names, colours and banner artwork can match your brand, and seasonal skins are available for the main holidays.
