Content ideas

What to make with the kit

Pick the format that suits your audience. Each one lists the assets that help and an angle you could use this month.

Written reviews

Readers want the facts first: RTP, volatility, paylines, maximum win and the bonus feature. The game sheet in the kit gives you all of them in one place, taken from the catalogue.

Assets that help
Cover, two or three screenshots, game sheet
Try this angle
Buffalo Quest publishes three RTP configurations (96.5%, 97% and 98%). Explain what that means for players and why the casino chooses one.

Live streams

Use the demo builds to show how a game plays without real-money stakes on screen. Wide banners from the kit make good offline and break screens.

Assets that help
Wide banner for your offline screen, logo, demo link
Try this angle
A keno night: play Super Wild Keno and Super Hexa Keno side by side and talk viewers through how the two differ.

How-to guides

Explain rules and features in plain language. Guides age well and bring steady search traffic. Stick to how a game works, never how to “beat” it.

Assets that help
Feature screenshots, rules text, glossary
Try this angle
“What is a buy feature?” using Super Keno, with terms linked to our glossary.

Release news

Be first with accurate launch coverage. The calendar above gives you the dates; the kit gives you approved art to publish on the day.

Assets that help
Cover, banner, release date, key specs
Try this angle
A first look at Golden Luck before 31 December: 25 paylines, medium-high volatility and a multiplier feature.

Short clips

Vertical clips of a feature landing or a theme reveal work well on social. Keep the 18+ label on screen and link to your full review.

Assets that help
Trailer cuts, cover art, logo
Try this angle
Fifteen seconds on Springtime Delight’s seasonal art, timed for spring content.

Responsible promotion

The rules we ask every partner to follow

These protect your audience and your reputation as much as ours. Content that breaks them is removed from the programme.

Our responsible gaming page

Do

  • Show an 18+ notice on every page, video and post that features our games.
  • Describe RTP as a long-term average across many plays, not a promise for one session.
  • Link to support for problem gambling where your audience lives.
  • Use approved artwork as supplied and follow the brand guidelines.
  • Say clearly when content is sponsored or contains affiliate links.

Don’t

  • Promise or imply wins, “systems” or ways to beat a game.
  • Present gambling as a way to earn money or solve money problems.
  • Target or appeal to anyone under 18, including through cartoon-led or school-themed content.
  • Show demo play as if it were real-money play, or edit win amounts.
  • Crop, recolour or redraw our logo or game art.

Questions

Affiliates, answered

Who can apply?

Content creators, review sites and affiliate partners who meet the programme terms and whose audience is over 18.

Where do I get assets?

Approved assets are shared once you join. The brand guidelines are public on the brand page.

How is performance tracked?

Tracking and terms are confirmed directly with the team before you publish.

Can I stream demo play?

Yes, as long as it is clearly labelled as demo play and you follow the responsible promotion rules on this page.

How do I find out about new releases?

Partners get the release calendar and an email when a date is set or moves. Public dates are also on each game page.

Can I quote RTP and max win figures?

Yes. Use the figures from the game sheet or the game page, and note that casinos may run different configurations.
