About us

Vegas Studio makes slots and keno, one world at a time, and builds the tools operators need to run them. This is how that work gets done.

Read the story

We are a game studio first. Every title in our catalogue was drawn, modelled, scored and tested by people who care how it feels on the fortieth spin, not just the first.

The catalogue runs from frontier slots like Buffalo Quest to the very high volatility of Super Hexa Keno. Alongside the games sit six operator solutions, from game aggregation to affiliate management, because a good game still needs somewhere to live and someone to find it.

What follows is not a company history. It is the way we work: the six stages every game passes through, what we hold ourselves to, and how we work with the operators, affiliates and media partners who take our games to players.

Six chapters

How a game gets made

01
Idea of 06

  1. Chapter 01

    Idea

    Every game starts as one sentence about how the player should feel.

    Before there is a theme, there is a moment we want to build towards: a wild that grows, a keno draw that keeps you leaning in, a bonus you can see coming. The theme is chosen to serve that moment, not the other way round.

    Ideas are pitched, argued over and often dropped. The ones that survive have a clear hook that a player could explain to a friend in a few words.

  2. Chapter 02

    Art

    A world you can read at a glance on a small screen.

    Our artists build each game as its own place, from the fairy glade of Fairy's Wish to the riveted towers of Steam Strikes. Then comes the harder part: making sure every symbol is recognisable at phone size, high-value symbols look valuable, and a win is obvious without a word of text.

    Animation is planned with the math, so the biggest moments get the most screen time.

  3. Chapter 03

    Math

    The numbers decide whether the game is fun for an hour or a minute.

    The model is built alongside the art, not after it. We set the return to player, the volatility band, the hit rate and the max win, then shape how the return is split between the base game and features. Golden Luck, for example, lists a 95% RTP, medium-high volatility and a 40% hit rate.

    Some games carry several RTP configurations so operators can pick the one their market needs. We also work with outside designers on their models; see math collaboration.

  4. Chapter 04

    Sound

    Half of a win is what you hear.

    Reel stops, anticipation on the last reel, a different sting for each win tier, a soundtrack that does not wear thin after twenty minutes. Rockstar Riffs leans on its guitar; a keno game needs something calmer that still builds tension as the balls drop.

    Every game is also checked with the sound off, because plenty of people play that way.

  5. Chapter 05

    Testing

    Millions of simulated rounds, then real people on real devices.

    Simulation confirms that the measured return matches the target and that features land as often as designed. Quality assurance then plays the game on phones, tablets and desktops, in each supported language, hunting for anything that breaks, overlaps or confuses.

    Where a market requires independent testing, the game is prepared for it with the documentation that goes alongside.

  6. Chapter 06

    Launch

    Release day is the start of the job, not the end.

    Operators get specification sheets, demo builds and artwork in the sizes their lobbies use. Our marketing tools and promo games help them introduce the game to players.

    After launch we watch how the game performs, answer partner questions and feed what we learn into the next idea.

What we hold to

Four values, written down so we can be held to them

  1. 01

    Craft

    Every game gets its own world, art, model and sound. We do not reskin a hit and call it new. If a game is not ready, it waits.

  2. 02

    Players first

    Rules should be clear, paytables easy to find and the odds honestly described. Fun that relies on confusion is not fun we want to make.

  3. 03

    Partnership

    Operators, affiliates and media partners know their players better than we do. We listen, share what we know and answer the awkward questions straight.

  4. 04

    Curiosity

    New mechanics, new formats, new ways to play keno. We try plenty of ideas, test them properly and keep only the ones that earn their place.

Our commitment

Responsible gaming is part of the design brief

Our games are for adults, and we build them to be enjoyed in moderation. That means clear rules and paytables, no design that hides what a player has spent, and support for the session tools operators provide. We expect the same care from the partners who offer our games.
