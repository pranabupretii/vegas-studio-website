The three deal types

Where $1,000 of revenue goes

Take ten new players who bring in $1,000 of net revenue in their first month. Here is how that month splits under each deal, before anyone plays a second month.

  • Revenue share

    Partner earns a percentage of net revenue for as long as their players stay. Rewards partners who send loyal players.

  • CPA

    A fixed payment for each player who deposits and passes checks. Predictable for partners, front-loaded for you.

  • Hybrid

    A smaller CPA plus a smaller revenue share. A common middle ground for partners who want some cash up front.

How checks work

Tracking

How a referral turns into a commission

Attribution is first click and permanent: once a player signs up through a partner, that player stays with that partner. Server-side tracking means blocked cookies do not lose the referral.

  1. Click

    A visitor clicks a partner's tracked link or banner.

  2. Tag

    The partner ID is stored with the visit, both in the browser and on our servers.

  3. Sign-up

    When the visitor registers, the account is tied to that partner for good.

  4. First deposit

    The deposit is checked. If it passes, the player qualifies for any CPA.

  5. Ledger

    Revenue from that player is shared every month, line by line.

Checked before anything is paid

  • Duplicate accounts

    Linked devices, payment methods and details are compared across accounts.

  • Self-referral

    Partners referring themselves or their own accounts are caught and excluded.

  • Bonus abuse

    Players who only chase bonuses do not count towards CPA.

  • Traffic quality

    Sudden spikes, odd sources and incentivised traffic are flagged for review.

Questions

Affiliate management, answered

Can partners see their own results?

Yes. Every partner gets their own dashboard with clicks, sign-ups, first deposits, active players and earnings, plus downloadable statements.

Can I run different deals for different partners?

Yes. Each partner can have their own plan, and you can run several plans side by side: revenue share, CPA, hybrid, or custom deals for a specific campaign or market.

How is a referral attributed?

First click and permanent. Once a player signs up through a partner's link, that player stays with that partner. Tracking runs on our servers as well as in the browser, so blocked cookies do not lose referrals.

What stops partners gaming CPA deals?

A player only qualifies after the first deposit passes duplicate-account, self-referral and bonus-abuse checks. You can also set a minimum deposit or activity level, and hold CPA until a review period ends.

Do you support sub-affiliates?

Yes. A partner can recruit other partners and earn a share of their commission, with the split set by you.

Does it connect to the casino platform?

Yes. It runs alongside the Vegas Studio casino platform, sharing the same player record and ledger. It can also connect to other platforms through our API.

Is this the same as the Vegas Studio affiliate programme?

No. This page is about software for operators to run their own programmes. If you are an affiliate who wants to promote our games, see the affiliates page.
