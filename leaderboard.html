How XP works

XP comes from taking part in the hub

Eligible activities on the official hub can award experience points, including quests and events. Your account shows the current tasks and progress. Which activities count, and how much each is worth, is set on the hub and can change.

  • Quests

    Short tasks listed in your account, such as exploring a feature or a new title. Each quest card shows what it asks for and what it awards.

  • Events

    Time-limited community activities. Dates, eligibility and any XP on offer are set out in each event's own terms.

  • Daily Reward

    A daily feature for eligible account holders. Sign in to check the current requirements and what is available.

  • Early Access

    Previews and demos of upcoming games. Eligibility, access and any rewards depend on the activity's terms.

Streaks explained

A streak counts days in a row

The streak next to an alias shows how many consecutive days that account has been active in the hub. In the snapshot, sk had a 2-day streak and camila a 1-day streak.

  • A streak is a count of days, not a score, and it has no effect on any game.
  • Missing a day usually starts the count again. The exact rule, including what counts as active and which time zone a day uses, is shown on the hub.
  • There is no need to keep a streak going. Skip days whenever you like.

Seasons and fair play

The ground rules, in general terms

The official hub terms always take priority. These points describe how community rankings like this one usually work.

  1. 01

    One person, one account

    Rankings are meant to compare people, so each player should hold a single account.

  2. 02

    Standings can change

    Positions move as activity happens. Any season or reset dates are announced on the hub, not on this page.

  3. 03

    Accounts can be reviewed

    Activity that breaks the hub terms, such as automated play or shared accounts, can lead to XP being removed or an account leaving the table.

  4. 04

    Terms come first

    If a season includes anything beyond the table itself, its eligibility and conditions are set out in its own terms. Nothing on this page is an offer.

  5. 05

    XP is not winnings

    XP reflects activity in Players Hub features. It does not affect how any game behaves or what it pays.

  6. 06

    Adults only

    Players Hub is for people aged 18 or over who meet the legal age requirement where they live.
