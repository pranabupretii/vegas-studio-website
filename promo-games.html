Scheduling

How often should players play?

Frequency matters more than prize size. Here is a week we often suggest as a starting point, with the wheel every day and two extra moments that give the week a shape.

Prize wheelYesYesYesYesYesYesYes

  • Once a day

    The default. Resets at midnight in the player’s time zone, so nobody feels they have missed out.

  • Streaks

    A small extra after three, five or seven days in a row. Keep the gap between the daily prize and the streak prize modest.

  • Event windows

    Open a format only for a weekend or a launch day. Scarcity works best when it is honest and the end time is shown.

  • Milestones

    One play to mark a birthday, an account anniversary or a new loyalty level, sent once and never repeated.

What you control

Your prizes, your rules

Every promo game runs from a short list of settings that your team can read at a glance. Nothing is hidden in the artwork.

  • Set the prize table and the chance of each prize, then see the expected cost per thousand plays before launch.
  • Cap the daily and total budget. When a cap is reached, remaining plays show a friendly message instead of a prize.
  • Choose who can play: by market, player status or campaign. Self-excluded players are always left out.
  • Show full terms and prize odds inside the game where your market asks for them.

Questions

Promo games, answered

Can promo games use my branding?

Yes. Any format can carry your logo, colours and campaign artwork, and we can build a permanent branded skin for your lobby.

How often can players play?

You choose. Once a day is the most common setting, with optional streak rewards and one-off plays for events or milestones.

What prizes can I offer?

Free spins, loyalty points, bonus games or other rewards that you define. You also set how likely each prize is.

Do players have to pay or stake anything?

No. Promo games are free plays. Players take part as a perk of having an account with you.

How do I stop costs running away?

Each game has daily and total budget caps. You can see the expected cost per thousand plays before anything goes live.

How quickly can a seasonal skin go live?

Ready-made skins are the quickest route. A brand-new skin takes longer because of the artwork, so we agree dates when we plan your year.

Ready to put a game in your lobby?

Tell us which format you like and when you want it live. We will send a mock-up in your colours.
