Your first 90 days

From first call to first review

A typical launch, step by step. The day markers are a guide; your plan is set together at kick-off.

  1. Day 1

    Kick-off call

    We learn your platform, markets and player base, and share the catalogue with release dates.

  2. Days 2–14

    Content list and terms

    Agree which games launch first, the RTP configuration for each market and the commercial terms.

  3. Days 15–45

    Integration and staging

    Your team connects to our API or your aggregator enables the games. We test together on staging.

  4. Days 46–60

    Market checks and sign-off

    Documents for each market, final build checks and approval from your compliance team.

  5. Days 61–75

    Go live

    Games go into the lobby with approved artwork, game sheets and copy ready for your CRM and social channels.

  6. Days 76–90

    First review

    We look at how the games are landing with your players and plan the next releases into your lobby.

What operators say

Notes from launch teams

Questions

For operators, answered

Something else on your list? Talk to the studio.

How do I get started?

Contact the studio. We will set up a call to understand your platform, your markets and the games you are interested in, then send a launch plan.

Can we take only a few games to begin with?

Yes. Many operators start with a handful of titles that suit their players and add more as releases come out. The catalogue currently has 19 games: 16 slots and 3 keno.

Which RTP configurations are available?

It depends on the game. Most titles publish a 95% configuration; Buffalo Quest is listed at 96.5%, 97% and 98%. Each game page shows the published options.

Do you provide marketing assets?

Yes. Each game comes with lobby artwork, screenshots, a game sheet and copy for your campaigns, delivered before go-live.

Can I get early access to new releases?

Talk to the team about upcoming releases and preview builds. Golden Luck, Super Keno and Buffalo Quest already have release dates.

Which markets and certifications do the games cover?

Certification details are shared on request. Tell us where you operate and we will confirm what is available for each game.

What happens after launch?

You keep the same contact. We review performance with you after the first weeks and plan which new releases to add to your lobby.
