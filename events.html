What to expect at a meeting

A typical forty-five minute session, minute by minute. We keep to time, and we send the follow-up the same week.

  1. 00:00

    Introductions

    Who is in the room and what each person looks after. No slides yet.

  2. 05:00

    Your goals

    You tell us what you are trying to do: fill a gap in the lobby, launch in a new market, add a promotion.

  3. 12:00

    Live walkthrough

    Games played live, or the integration flow shown on screen. You steer; we skip whatever is not relevant.

  4. 30:00

    Questions

    Specs, configurations, certification, timelines. If we do not know an answer, we say so and come back with it.

  5. 40:00

    Next steps

    We agree what happens next and who owns it, then close on time.

  6. After

    Follow-up

    A short written summary with links, documents and the actions we agreed.

Travel and online

On a screen or in the same room

Online, most weeks

Video calls are the default. We send a calendar invite with a link, work across time zones and share the screen so you can see builds running rather than slides about them.

  • Tell us your time zone when you book
  • Any participants welcome, from product to compliance
  • We only record with everyone's agreement

In person, by arrangement

We travel for partner meetings and attend industry shows during the year. The shows change from year to year, so ask us which ones we are planning to attend and we will try to set a time while we are there.

  • Give us a few weeks' notice for travel
  • Meet at your office or a venue we agree
  • Bring the people who will make the decision

Good questions

Events FAQ

Something else? Ask the team.

How do I book a meeting?

Send a message through the contact page with the meeting type, two or three dates that suit you and your time zone. We will reply to confirm a time.

Do I need to be an existing partner?

No. Demos and first conversations are open to any operator, aggregator, affiliate or studio with a real project in mind.

Can we meet at a trade show?

Often, yes. Our attendance changes each year, so ask which shows we are planning to attend and we will try to find a slot.

Can you join a call with our technical team only?

Yes. Integration walkthroughs work best with the engineers who will do the work, and we will bring ours.

Is the agenda on this page fixed?

No. It shows the formats we run regularly. Dates and times are always confirmed directly with you.

Will you share materials afterwards?

Yes. You receive a short summary with links, specification sheets or documents relevant to what we discussed.

Booking request

Reserve your slot

Use the contact page and include these four things. That is all we need to find the right people and a time that works.

  1. Meeting type Demo, integration or partnership
  2. Dates Two or three options, with your time zone
  3. Who is joining Names and roles from your side
  4. Online or in person And the city, if in person
