Same day, different stories

Why the two lists differ

An amount on its own says nothing about the stake behind it. A large payout can come from a high stake and a modest multiplier. A large multiplier can come from a small stake and pay a modest amount. So the two rankings pick out different results.

Behind the list

How wins are recorded

A top-wins list is a ranking of finished results over a set period. The official hub sets the exact rules for what is counted. In outline, it works like this.

  1. 01

    A round finishes

    The game's random number generator decides the outcome and the round settles.

  2. 02

    The result is logged

    The game, the amount won and the stake are recorded with the time of the round.

  3. 03

    Results are ranked

    Within the captured period, here one day, results are sorted by amount and, separately, by multiplier.

  4. 04

    A snapshot is published

    This page shows the snapshot of 25 November 2026. The live hub shows the current period.

$16.00 won÷$0.50 staked=32×

Reading a multiplier, with invented numbers: a $16.00 win on a $0.50 stake is 32 times the stake, so it reads as 32×. The same 32× on a $5.00 stake would pay $160.00. The multiplier is identical; only the amount changes.

Please read

Past results don't predict future results

Every result on this page was decided at random. A game that appears here is not "hot", and a game that does not is not "due".

  • Each round stands alone. The random number generator has no memory of earlier rounds or earlier days.
  • Big wins are rare by design. Most rounds pay less than the stake or nothing at all. The list shows the exceptions, not what usually happens.
  • The list is not a recommendation. It records what happened. It is not advice on what to play or how much to stake.
