Players Hub

What is Players Hub?

A place to explore games, follow community activity and track your progress. Sign in on the official hub for quests, rewards and available early-access demos.

Is this website the same as the official hub?

No. This is the Vegas Studio website, where you can read about our games and see published snapshots. Accounts, quests and progress live on the official hub at players.vegas.studio.

Do I need an account to use these pages?

No. Game pages, the RTP and top-win snapshots and the leaderboard snapshot are open to anyone who meets the age requirement. Quests, progress and early access need a Players Hub account.

How up to date are the figures on this site?

Each snapshot shows the date it was captured, for example the leaderboard is a public snapshot from 25 November 2026. They are not live feeds. The official hub carries current information.

Can I preview upcoming games?

The official Early Access section lists available previews and demos. Eligibility, access and any rewards depend on the activity’s terms.

Games

Where can I play Vegas Studio games?

Vegas Studio makes games and supplies them to operators. Whether a game is available to you depends on the operator and the rules where you live. Browse the titles on our games page.

What does “Upcoming” mean on a game?

The game has been announced but is not yet released. Where a planned release date is published we show it, but dates can move.

Why does a game list more than one RTP?

Some games are built with several RTP configurations, and the operator chooses which one to offer. Check the game’s information screen where you play to see the setting in use.

Where do I find a game’s rules and paytable?

Inside the game, in its help or information screen. Our game pages summarise the published specifications, such as volatility and paylines, where they are available.

What is volatility?

Volatility describes how wins tend to be spread out. Lower volatility usually means smaller, more frequent wins; higher volatility usually means fewer, larger ones. It describes a pattern over many rounds, not what will happen next. See the glossary.

RTP and results

What is RTP?

Theoretical return to player describes a game’s expected return across a very large number of plays. Short-term observed RTP can differ significantly. Neither predicts an individual result.

What does Live RTP show?

Observed RTP figures measured over a recent sample of play, published as dated snapshots on Live RTP. Over short periods these can sit well above or below the theoretical figure. They do not suggest a game is “due” to pay.

Do recent top wins mean a game is more likely to pay?

No. Each round is independent, so a large win on a game says nothing about the next round. Top wins are a record of what happened, not a forecast.

Are game results random?

Each round’s outcome is decided by a random number generator (RNG), independently of earlier rounds. Past results, rankings and RTP figures cannot be used to predict the next result. See RNG in the glossary.

What is hit rate?

Hit rate is the share of rounds that return any win over the long run. Many of those wins can be smaller than the stake, so a high hit rate does not mean a player comes out ahead. See hit rate.

Account and rewards

What is the Daily Reward?

The official hub offers eligible account holders a daily reward feature. Sign in to check the current requirements and available rewards.

How do I earn XP?

Eligible activities on the official hub can award experience points, including quests and events. Your account shows the current tasks and progress.

What is a streak?

A count of consecutive days an account has been active in the hub. It has no effect on any game. The exact rule for what counts as a day is shown on the hub.

How does the leaderboard work?

Accounts are ranked by XP. Our leaderboard page shows a dated public snapshot; the live table and your own position are on the official hub.

I can’t sign in to Players Hub. What should I do?

Use the help options on the official hub sign-in page. We can’t see or change hub accounts from this website, and we will never ask for your password. For general questions, use our contact page.

How do I change or close my hub account?

Account settings and requests are handled on the official hub once you sign in. The privacy policy explains how personal data is handled.

Responsible play

How do I set limits or take a break?

The operator where you play provides tools such as deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion. Set them before you start, and use them whenever you need to. Our responsible gaming page has more guidance.

Where can I get help if gambling stops being fun?

Stop playing and seek support through your operator’s responsible-gaming tools. Our responsible gaming page sets out next steps. Talking to someone you trust can help too.

Should I play to climb the leaderboard or keep a streak?

There is no need. Rankings and streaks are a light community feature. Play only with time and money you can afford, and never to chase a position or win back losses.

Is Players Hub open to under-18s?

No. Players Hub and our games are for adults aged 18 or over who meet the legal age requirement where they live.

Keep play in perspective

Our content is for adults who meet the legal age requirement where they live. Game results are uncertain. Historical results, rankings and short-term RTP figures do not predict your next result.

  • Decide on a time and money limit before you start.
  • Take regular breaks, and stop if it stops being enjoyable.
  • Never treat play as a way to recover losses or earn an income.
  • Use your operator’s tools: deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion.
